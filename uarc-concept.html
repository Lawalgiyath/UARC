I. Conference Theme

Why Sustainable Frontiers

Food systems, public health, housing and human security are converging crises rather than separate fields. The 19th edition asks researchers across the sciences, humanities and policy schools to bring evidence to bear on problems that no single discipline can solve alone, and to publish that evidence where it can be used.

II. Subthemes

Eight tracks for the call for papers

Every submission is assigned to one track at the point of submission. Reviewers and session chairs are organised along these same lines, so choose the track closest to your contribution.

I

Cultural Heritage, Human Rights, Humanities and Sustainable Development

Indigenous knowledge systems, rights based approaches and the humanities in service of inclusive development.

II

Biotechnology and Genomic Innovations for Food and Health Security

Applied genomics, crop science and biotechnology addressing nutrition and disease burden.

III

AI, Data Science, Cybersecurity and Clean Tech Innovation

Computational and digital approaches to infrastructure, information security and low carbon technology.

IV

Leadership and Policy Strategies for Sustainable Development

Governance frameworks and institutional leadership models aligned to the Sustainable Development Goals.

V

Public Health, Drug Discovery, Therapeutics and Global Preparedness

Pharmaceutical research, epidemiology and health systems readiness for emerging threats.

VI

Transformative Education and Capacity Building for Future Sustainability

Pedagogical innovation and skills development for a changing labour market.

VII

Smart Cities, Human Centered Urban Design and Environmental Resilience

Urban planning, housing and infrastructure that withstand climate and population pressure.

VIII

Social Policy and Human Security in Emerging Economies

Social protection, conflict prevention and human security frameworks in developing economies.

IX. Questions

Frequently asked

What format should my abstract follow?
Between 300 and 500 words, structured under Background, Methods, Results and Conclusion, submitted as a Word or PDF document through the portal above.
Will accepted abstracts be published?
Abstracts accepted for oral or poster presentation will be considered for publication in the University of Lagos Journals, subject to that journal's own peer review process.
Can international presenters get a letter of invitation?
Yes. Once registration and payment are confirmed, an invitation letter for visa purposes can be requested from the Secretariat by email.
What is the refund policy?
Registration fees are non refundable after 6 October 2026, but are fully transferable to another delegate from the same institution at no charge.
Is this edition in person?
Yes, all sessions run in person at the Jelili Adebisi Omotola Hall. Contact the Secretariat directly if you require special accommodation.
How do I track my submission status?
Use the reference code emailed and texted to you at the time of submission in the tracker beneath the abstract portal, or contact the Secretariat directly.
